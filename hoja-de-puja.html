What it does not do

It cannot see structural damage. Damage under the floor or in the rails is in no data source anyone sells, which is exactly why you inspect. Nothing here returns a clean verdict on a car. It ranks, it prices, and it says plainly where it is guessing.

It never bids for you, never signs into Copart, Manheim, Carfax or eBay on your behalf, and never copies their photographs. Those lines protect your auction access, which is the one thing here you could not rebuild.

The demo data is invented. The 800 lots and the six Ledger units exist to show the shape of the thing with history inside it. The Copart fee table is a starting point until one of your real invoices replaces it.
